steps in RDD computation:
-------------------------
step 1: Initially all the RDDs are just declared
        we load and execute them on demand by performing an action
        when action is performed,flow execution will start from its root RDD
        or availabe RDD
        This process is called as Lazy Evolution

step 2: During the flow execution,Each RDD partitions data will be loaded into 
        RAms(memory) and computed in RAM
        here RDDs will be loaded sequentially one after the another

step 3:Once Rdd computation is finished,it waits for its next RDD,
       if next RDD is ready then previous RDD will be removed from the Ram.
       This process continues untill the flow or action is completed


step 4::when action is performed,all RDDs are strored and computed at spark side
       (i.e spark cluster machines)
       but the final result that we get by performing action is collected and
       stored in the client machine
       client means the machine from where the job is submitted

step 5:During the flow execution,the RDD whose o/p  is commonly used by
       mutiple flows will be persisted

step 6: Finally the RDD sored in RAM can be un-persisted if no longer required
