Q)what are the situations where RDD will be removed from the Ram(memory).,
Ans) 1.whenever next RDD is ready, previous RDD will be removed from the RAM
        2.when action or execution is completed, last RDD will be removed from the RAM.

Q)Generally which RDD will be persisted?
Ans)The RDD whose o/p is commonly used by multiple flows that RDD will be persisted

Q)what is Lazy Evolution in spark?
Ans) Executing the dataflow on demand whenever action is performed ,such kind of evolution we call it as 
        Lazy evolution. 
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Steps in RDD Computation:

step 1: Initially all the RDDs are just declared, they are not strored anywhere or not computed,
           we load and execute them on demand by performing an action.
            when action is performed, flow execution will start from its root RDD (or) available RDD in RAM

step2: During flow Execution, Each RDD partitions data will be loaded into RAms and computed in
       RAM.
          here RDDs will be loaded sequentially one after the another,

step3 :Once RDD computation is finished,it waits for its next RDD . 
       If next RDD is ready then previous RDD will be removed from the RAM.
       This process continues untill the action is completed.

step 4:when action is performed, all RDDs are stored and computed at 
       spark side(i.e in spark cluster machines)
       but the final result that we get by performing action is collected and 
       stored in the client machine.
           client means the machine from where the job is submitted.

step 5: During the flow execution, the RDD o/p which is commonly used by
        multiple flows will be persisted.

step 6: Finally the RDD stored in RAM can be unpersisted if it is no 
        longer required.
