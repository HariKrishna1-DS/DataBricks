

cluster types:
1.All-purpose cluster: manual scaling
                       configured with diff amount of compute resources
                       These clusters can be created manually and are
                       persistent--->they can be terminated and restarted
                       at anytime


2.Job cluster:for running automated tasks--->such as ETL pipelines
              job clusters are generated--->when a job begins execution and
              are terminated automatically when the job is complete
              They cannot be re-started

Feature               All-purpose cluster          Job cluster
-------               -------------------          ------------
Creation    -------->manual(UI,CLI or RESTAPI)---->Automatic(by job scheduler)
Termination--------->manual(can be restarted)----->Automatic(terminated after job)
Scalability--------->Manual scaling possible ----->Not scalable
Cost --------------->charge high             ----->lower cost
No of users---------->Multiple users         ----->single job
usecase ------------->Interactive analysis   ----->scheduled jobs 
                      collaboration                batch processing
